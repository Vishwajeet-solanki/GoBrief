FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is a GoBrief summary?

A careful distillation of a nonfiction book's core arguments, stories and takeaways — about 15 minutes to read or listen to. It's the fastest honest way to know whether a book deserves your next 10 hours.

What do I get on the free plan?

The entire catalog is browsable — every title, description, track, plan and “who should read this” guide. A rotating selection of summaries is fully unlocked (text and audio), and features like streaks, highlights and quizzes work on everything you can access.

Can I listen offline?

Yes. Premium members can download any summary's audio and text for fully offline listening — flights, subways, mountains, wherever.

How are books chosen and summarized?

We stock the shelves with books people actually recommend — enduring bestsellers and modern classics across 15 categories — and hold every summary to the same bar: faithful to the author, ruthless with fluff.

Do summaries replace the real books?

No — they're a map, not the territory. Every book page links to the full book, and our favorite outcome is you falling in love with one and buying it.

How do I delete my account or data?

Anytime, in-app or via our account deletion page. Your data, your call.
