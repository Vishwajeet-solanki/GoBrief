## What is GoBrief?

GoBrief is a reading and learning app that helps you explore big ideas in less time. Each title is distilled into a focused experience you can **read** or **listen to**—perfect for commutes, coffee breaks, or a quiet evening when you still want to feel like you moved forward.

Pick up where you left off, follow a clear path through topics that matter to you, and set goals so your home screen reflects *your* journey—not a generic feed. Challenges and achievements turn consistency into something you can actually see and celebrate.

We built GoBrief for people who want **depth without overwhelm**: a calm, modern home for curiosity, habit, and real progress.

---

## What’s inside

GoBrief ships with a **curated catalog** you can start exploring right away:

- **19 hand-picked titles** — distilled ideas you can read or listen to, built for busy days  
- **6 topic areas** — browse by the kind of growth you want, not endless scrolling  

**Those topics span:** Business & career · Happiness · Money & investment · Productivity · Self-growth · Spirituality  

We keep **growing the library** and deepening each journey—so expect more to discover over time.

## Designed to feel effortless

GoBrief is built to get out of your way. A calm, modern interface, a player that keeps your place across reading and listening, and a home screen that adapts to *your* goals instead of an endless feed.

- **Seamless read ↔ listen** — switch between text and audio without losing your spot
- **Offline-ready** — download titles and keep learning with no signal
- **Progress that syncs** — your library, bookmarks, and highlights follow you across devices
- **Goals, challenges & achievements** — turn consistency into visible momentum
- **Built-in player** — background audio, playlists, and smooth playback controls

---

## Built with care

A polished consumer app deserves a solid foundation. GoBrief is engineered for performance, reliability, and security from day one.

- **Modern, native-grade stack** — React Native 0.81 + Expo SDK 54, written end-to-end in **TypeScript**
- **Smooth by design** — Reanimated-powered motion, NativeWind styling, and a custom audio engine built on `react-native-track-player`
- **Cloud-backed** — Firebase Auth, Firestore, Crashlytics, and Cloud Functions keep data in sync and the app stable in production
- **Secure content delivery** — protected media with App Check and a hardened delivery pipeline
- **Battle-tested** — **100+ automated test suites** guard the experience against regressions
- **Production builds** — shipped to Android via EAS Build
