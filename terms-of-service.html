2. Description of the Services

GoBrief is an Android audiobook application that delivers book summaries with text-to-speech narration, organized into curated categories and multi-day Growth Plans. The Services include:

  • Audio Playback: Streaming and offline playback of audiobook content with adjustable playback speeds (0.5x to 2x).
  • Text-to-Speech Generation: Audio generation using advanced TTS models (Sherpa ONNX) with customizable voice selections per book or section.
  • Offline Downloads: Download audio content for offline listening where permitted.
  • Cloud Sync: Synchronization of reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, playlists, and preferences across your devices via Firebase Firestore.
  • Reading Progress: Track your listening progress with automatic position syncing and resume capability.
  • Bookmarks and Highlights: Create and manage bookmarks and highlights within books.
  • Sleep Timer: Set a sleep timer to automatically stop playback after a specified duration.
  • Achievements and Growth Plans: Gamification features including reading streaks, achievements, and multi-day Growth Plans (challenges).
  • Quizzes: Optional quiz features to test your comprehension of book content.
  • Subscription Features: "Bookiepia Pro" premium subscription providing access to enhanced features and content.
